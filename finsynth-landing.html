Permission-gated writes

FinSynth proposes. You confirm.

The agent never writes to your spreadsheet without explicit approval. It stages a proposal — value, cell, source — and waits. No unintended model changes.

Excel operations run locally via Office.js. FinSynth's backend never reads or stores your spreadsheet files.
